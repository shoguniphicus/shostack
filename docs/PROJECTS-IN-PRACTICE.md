# Products in practice — business context, not proprietary implementation

ShoStack is informed by product-building work across different problem types. These are **public product contexts**, not testimonials, revenue or performance claims, and not proof that an identical harness was installed in every project.

| Product | Public context | The transferable engineering question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Alamak.my](https://alamak.my/) | Malay-language learning content and experiences | How do changing content, learning flows and release decisions stay consistent and reviewable? |
| [Language-Gym.com](https://language-gym.com/) | Long-lived digital-learning SaaS | How do changing applications preserve compatibility and operational correctness? |
| [AussieCampervans.com](https://aussiecampervans.com/) | Customer-facing travel services and digital systems | How can business workflows and integrations remain reliable and understandable to operators? |
| [HiggsOcean](https://github.com/shoguniphicus/higgsocean) | AI-native creative software in active alpha development | How do frontend/backend contracts, agent work and architecture records avoid drifting apart? |

**Status precision:** HiggsOcean is not presented as a completed, end-to-end production SaaS. These examples establish breadth of context only; no independently verified uptime, scale, business valuation, exclusivity of authorship or customer financial claim is made.

Methods are described in [the SaaS delivery system](SAAS-DELIVERY-SYSTEM.md), with evidence evaluated by [the ShoStack standard](EVIDENCE.md). Client/private source, internal topology, proprietary prompts, customer data and distinctive algorithms never belong in these examples.
